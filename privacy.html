Privacy policy

Channel authorization and drafts

The inbox tool uses TikTok authorization to check the connected username and send the video selected by the channel editor to TikTok. The tool stores the access credential on the production computer. A local delivery log stores a video fingerprint, a TikTok delivery identifier and its status so the same draft can be recognized on a retry.

TikTok receives the video to deliver the draft to the creator. The operator chooses whether to publish it in TikTok. TikTok handles media received on its platform under its own privacy policy.

Website visits

These pages contain no contact form, advertising or analytics scripts. GitHub Pages, the website host, logs visitors’ IP addresses for security. GitHub Pages data collection.

Disconnecting or requesting deletion

The operator can revoke app access in TikTok and remove the saved credential and delivery records from the production computer. Drafts already sent can be managed in TikTok. For questions about data or to request deletion of local records, contact the operator. Contact GTA VI Clips on Instagram.
